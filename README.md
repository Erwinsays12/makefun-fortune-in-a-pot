A kind, funny fortune for the houseplant on the windowsill, entered in
[Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/c/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9)
on make.fun.

## The fortune

> Your future holds long sunbeams, a full watering can, and a keeper who
> says sorry before every little haircut.

One line, 110 characters: a prediction with the cadence of a fortune, a
small joke a plant lover would laugh at (the keeper apologizing before
every pruning "haircut"), and nothing but warmth for the plant and its
keeper — no doom, no withering, no mockery.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes that exact line to
`/output/entry.txt`. It is fully deterministic: no input, no clock, no
network and no randomness, so two runs always produce byte-identical
output.
